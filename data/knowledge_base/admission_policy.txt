Hospital Admission Policy

Required documents
Patients should bring a government-issued photo identity document, the referral or appointment document when available, current prescriptions, and relevant previous medical reports. Children may use a birth certificate together with the parent or guardian's identity document. Insurance patients should also bring their insurance card and authorisation documents.

Admission desk
The admission desk is located near the main entrance and operates twenty-four hours for emergency admissions. Planned admissions should report at the time mentioned in the admission confirmation.

Valuables
Patients are advised not to bring jewellery, large amounts of cash, or other valuables. The hospital is not responsible for personal items that are not deposited through an approved valuables process.
