Hospital Visiting Hours

General wards
General ward visiting time is from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM. A maximum of two visitors may enter for one patient at a time.

Critical-care areas
Critical-care visiting is controlled by the unit and may be shorter or restricted according to the patient's condition and infection-control requirements. Visitors should ask the unit desk before entering.

Visitor safety
Visitors with fever, cough, vomiting, diarrhoea, or another potentially infectious illness should postpone the visit. Children may be restricted in some clinical areas.
