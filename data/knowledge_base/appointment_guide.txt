Hospital Appointment Guide

Booking
Appointments can be requested through the hospital appointment desk, the patient portal, or the published hospital telephone number. The patient should provide a name, contact number, preferred department, and a brief reason for the visit.

Arrival
Patients should arrive fifteen minutes before the scheduled appointment for registration. New patients should carry an identity document and relevant previous reports.

Cancellation
If a patient cannot attend, the appointment should be cancelled or rescheduled as early as possible so the slot can be offered to another patient.
